About the studio

Grinding Mind Games is a new indie studio in Costa Rica building video games.

We're at the research and prototyping stage. Our first game will be a 2D title for Steam, built with the Godot engine. Before we settle on a concept, we're building small prototypes to learn the engine and the platform.

We're also studying what research says about how games engage the brain, and we plan to build that into our first release.

Founder

Gilbert has spent more than 20 years in the software industry and works in cloud solutions today. Grinding Mind Games is where he is building the games he has always wanted to make.
